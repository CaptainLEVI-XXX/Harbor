How Harbor earns

Harbor buys ETH-backed assets — wstETH, rETH, cbETH — and pending Lido withdrawal receipts, at a discount to what they eventually settle at. It holds them until they settle at full value. The gap is the yield.

Sellers get their ETH immediately instead of waiting out a withdrawal queue. Harbor takes on the wait, and depositors are paid for carrying it.

Your deposit is pooled with everyone else's. hWETH is your share of the pool, and it is worth more WETH over time rather than paying anything out.
